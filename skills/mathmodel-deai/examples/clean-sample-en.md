The scheduler cut average bus dwell time from 42 s to 31 s across 18 junctions, a 26% reduction measured over four weeks
of automatic vehicle location data. We fit a gradient-boosted model on 320 morning-peak records and chose
hyperparameters by five-fold cross-validation; the mean absolute percentage error is 8.7%, against 13.2% for the
seasonal baseline. Under a plus or minus 10% flow perturbation the error moves by at most 0.6 percentage points.

The estimate assumes complete detector coverage, which does not hold on the two northern corridors, so the result
applies to the instrumented area only. Extending it to the uncovered corridors would need loop detectors or probe
vehicle traces, and we did not have either.
